Not a forecast

A conditional pathway model, not a forecast. It shows which further steps would have to occur for a demonstrated capability to reach a particular consequence, and where safeguards could stop that pathway. It does not estimate probability.

Method and limitations

It cannot establish how probable a pathway is, the expected severity of an outcome, which safeguard is most cost-effective, how much a law would reduce risk, or that a consequence the pathway reaches under some settings will be reached in reality. Where the pathway does not reach a consequence, that is reported as does not occur under the current scenario settings, never as impossible. Governance options are stylised packages; no enacted statute is named or represented. Where the reviewed sources are silent, the model says unknown — it never reads silence as absence of a control. Sources: the Hugging Face technical timeline, the METR/Redwood investigation, and OpenAI's own account, the last of which is an interested party reporting retrospective internal analysis.
